## Cities

### Tokyo, Japan

**Duration:** 5 days

**Key Attractions:**
- Shibuya Crossing
- Senso-ji Temple
- Tokyo Skytree
- Meiji Shrine
- Ueno Park for cherry blossoms

**Why Visit:** As Japan's vibrant capital, Tokyo offers a mix of traditional and ultra-modern attractions. During cherry blossom season, the city's parks transform into pink wonderlands, creating a magical atmosphere. Tokyo presents the perfect blend of ancient traditions and futuristic technology.

### Kyoto, Japan

**Duration:** 4 days

**Key Attractions:**
- Fushimi Inari Shrine
- Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)
- Arashiyama Bamboo Grove
- Philosopher's Path for cherry blossoms

**Why Visit:** Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart with numerous temples, traditional gardens, and geisha districts. With over 1,600 Buddhist temples and 400 Shinto shrines, it offers an authentic glimpse into Japan's cultural heritage. The city is especially beautiful during cherry blossom season.

### Osaka, Japan

**Duration:** 2 days

**Key Attractions:**
- Osaka Castle
- Dotonbori district
- Kuromon Market
- Osaka Aquarium

**Why Visit:** Nearby Kyoto, Osaka offers amazing food, a vibrant atmosphere, and serves as a good base for a day trip to Nara. Known as "Japan's kitchen," it's the perfect place to enjoy street food and experience the energetic local culture.
